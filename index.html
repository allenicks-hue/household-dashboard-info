A Family Home Dashboard

A private, self-hosted family calendar and information display.

This is a personal project built for household use — a wall-mounted dashboard that pulls in our family calendar, weather, chores, and other day-to-day information onto a single shared screen.

It's not a public product or service — just a small self-hosted app running on our own hardware at home.
